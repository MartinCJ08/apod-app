# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2026

Barranca Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates this application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

## Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from users.

The App does not require account creation, user registration, or login credentials.

## Data Storage

Users may save their favorite Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) entries within the App. These saved items are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to Barranca Labs or any third-party servers.

## Internet Access

The App requires internet access solely to retrieve content from NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) API.

No personal information is sent to Barranca Labs when accessing this content.

## Third-Party Services

The App retrieves publicly available astronomy content from NASA's APOD API.

NASA may maintain its own privacy practices and policies. Users can review NASA's privacy information at:

https://www.nasa.gov/privacy/

The App does not use:

* Advertising services
* Analytics services
* User tracking technologies
* Firebase services
* Third-party authentication providers

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Because no personal information is collected, stored, or processed, the App presents minimal privacy risks to users of all ages.

## Future Changes

Future versions of the App may introduce new features, such as user accounts or cloud synchronization. If any feature requires the collection or processing of personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before such features become available.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App and/or on the page where this Privacy Policy is published.
